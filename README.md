# 13.0 — Hyperbolic Hashimoto  
A generalisation of Projects 5 and 8 into **mixed‑curvature, non‑uniform lattices**, integrating symbolic lattice specification with **curvature‑weighted non‑backtracking operators** and **anisotropic hyperbolic‑type dynamical evolution**.

---

## Hypothesis Framework

### **1. Research Hypothesis (H₁)**  
Embedding non‑backtracking operators into **mixed‑curvature lattices** (Euclidean + hyperbolic + anisotropic regions) produces **distinct spectral and dynamical behaviors**, including:

- curvature‑dependent spectral gaps,  
- anisotropic propagation modes, and  
- hyperbolic‑type exponential divergence patterns  

that cannot be reproduced by flat‑curvature Hashimoto operators.

This asserts that curvature weighting fundamentally reshapes non‑backtracking dynamics.

---

### **2. Null Hypothesis (H₀)**  
Curvature‑weighted non‑backtracking operators exhibit **no significant spectral or dynamical differences** from standard Hashimoto operators on uniform Euclidean lattices.

Under H₀, curvature does not meaningfully affect:

- eigenvalue distributions,  
- propagation anisotropy, or  
- dynamical stability.

---

### **3. Alternative Hypothesis (H₂)**  
Mixed‑curvature weighting **destabilizes** non‑backtracking dynamics, producing:

- chaotic propagation,  
- unstable spectral signatures, or  
- non‑interpretable anisotropic flows  

that reduce analytic tractability compared to Euclidean operators.

---

## 5. Experimental Method

1. **Symbolic Lattice Specification**  
   Construct mixed‑curvature lattices using symbolic geometry descriptors.

2. **Curvature‑Weighted Hashimoto Operator**  
   Define operator:  
   \[
   B_{\kappa} = W_{\kappa} \, B
   \]  
   where \( W_{\kappa} \) encodes curvature weights.

3. **Anisotropic Hyperbolic Dynamics**  
   Evolve states via:  
   \[
   x_{t+1} = f(B_{\kappa} x_t)
   \]  
   with hyperbolic‑type anisotropic update rules.

4. **Spectral & Dynamical Analysis**  
   - Compute eigenvalues and spectral gaps  
   - Measure divergence rates  
   - Detect anisotropic propagation modes  

5. **Comparative Evaluation**  
   Compare mixed‑curvature results to Euclidean baselines from Projects 5 and 8.
